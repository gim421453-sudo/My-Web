## 7. Authoritative ListCollectionIds IAM contract

The user-provided externally verified Google Cloud contract is accepted for
method-level policy design:

```text
LIST_COLLECTION_IDS_REQUIRED_PERMISSION = datastore.entities.list
METHOD_PERMISSION_CLASS = DATA_READ
REFERENCE_READ_ROLE = roles/datastore.viewer
```

`roles/datastore.viewer` includes `datastore.entities.list`. The role also
includes read capabilities such as `datastore.entities.get`, but
`ListCollectionIds` itself does not require `get` under the supplied contract.
No larger permission set is added.

The supplied role contract excludes these entity mutations:

```text
datastore.entities.create
datastore.entities.update
datastore.entities.delete
```

Therefore the predefined role itself is not entity-write capable. That does not
prove that an unknown service account has no other role.

```text

DATA_READ_ROLE_EVIDENCE = no exact audit-SA role binding recorded locally
```

counted as Firestore access. Any required control-plane request must receive its
